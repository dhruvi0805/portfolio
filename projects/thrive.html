Thrive

Mental wellness platform designed for young adults

Role

UX Designer

Timeline

12 weeks

Platform

Web & Mobile

Executive Summary

A comprehensive mental wellness platform designed specifically for young adults aged 18-25. The project focused on creating an accessible, stigma-free environment that provides resources, community support, and personalized wellness tracking to help users thrive mentally and emotionally.

Problem

Young adults face unique mental health challenges but often avoid seeking help due to stigma, cost, and lack of accessible resources. Existing mental health apps were either too clinical or too superficial, failing to address the specific needs of this demographic in an engaging, non-intimidating way.

Research

Conducted extensive research including 32 interviews with young adults, mental health professionals, and university counselors. Key insights revealed: users desired anonymity and privacy; peer support was as valuable as professional guidance; gamification elements increased engagement with wellness activities.

Design Decisions

We chose a warm, approachable visual design with soft colors and friendly illustrations to reduce clinical feel. The architecture balances self-service resources with community features. Privacy controls were made prominent and transparent. We incorporated gentle gamification to encourage daily engagement without overwhelming users.

Solution

A holistic wellness platform featuring mood tracking, guided exercises, peer support communities, and professional resources. The design emphasizes privacy, accessibility, and gentle engagement. Key features include personalized wellness journeys, anonymous community forums, and integration with university counseling services.

Impact

The platform achieved a 30% increase in user engagement compared to existing solutions. Users reported improved mood awareness and reduced stigma around mental health discussions. The anonymous community feature became particularly valuable, with over 1,000 daily active users sharing experiences and support.

Reflection

This project highlighted the importance of sensitive design in mental health spaces. Balancing professional credibility with approachability required careful consideration of every design element. The biggest learning was that small design details could significantly impact users' willingness to engage with mental health resources.
