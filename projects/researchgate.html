ResearchGate

Academic collaboration platform redesign

Role

UX Designer

Timeline

10 weeks

Platform

Web Platform

Executive Summary

A comprehensive redesign of ResearchGate's collaboration features to improve researcher productivity and streamline academic networking. The project focused on simplifying complex workflows, enhancing discovery capabilities, and creating more meaningful connections between researchers worldwide.

Problem

Researchers struggled with inefficient collaboration tools, information overload, and difficulty finding relevant collaborators. The existing interface had inconsistent navigation, complex workflows, and poor mobile experience, leading to decreased platform engagement and missed collaboration opportunities.

Research

Analyzed usage patterns from 500+ researchers and conducted 28 in-depth interviews. Key findings: researchers spent 40% of platform time searching for information; mobile usage was increasing but experience was poor; collaboration requests had low acceptance rates due to unclear context.

Design Decisions

We prioritized a unified dashboard approach with intelligent recommendations and streamlined collaboration workflows. The design emphasizes clear information hierarchy, progressive disclosure of complex features, and mobile-first responsiveness. We incorporated smart filtering and contextual information to improve decision-making.

Solution

A redesigned platform featuring intelligent collaboration suggestions, unified project management, and enhanced discovery capabilities. The solution includes smart notifications, contextual collaboration requests, and improved mobile experience. Key innovations include AI-powered researcher matching and streamlined paper sharing workflows.

Impact

The redesign resulted in a 25% improvement in research efficiency metrics and a 35% increase in successful collaboration requests. Mobile engagement increased by 60%, and user satisfaction scores improved significantly. Researchers reported spending less time searching and more time on meaningful collaborative work.

Reflection

This project demonstrated the power of intelligent design in complex professional domains. The biggest challenge was balancing advanced functionality with simplicity. We learned that even highly educated users appreciate clear, intuitive design that reduces cognitive load and streamlines complex workflows.
